I grew up in Western Massachusetts and studied Informatics at UMass Amherst, a program that combines art and computer science. Since 2019 I've worked as a web developer, building sites and applications where design and code meet. Away from the screen, I ride my bike, read science fiction, and watch movies at the Coolidge Corner Theater.
