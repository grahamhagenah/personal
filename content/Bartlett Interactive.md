From 2019 to 2021 I was a web developer and designer at Bartlett Interactive, a small agency in Concord, Massachusetts, that builds websites for nonprofits and local organizations.

## What I did

- **Delivered more than 30 web projects** for Boston-area nonprofits, often on tight deadlines.
- **Ran projects from start to finish:** client consultations, scoping and budgets, then design, development and launch.
- **Built responsive, CMS-driven sites** in WordPress with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP, with custom front-end components and content that clients could edit themselves.
- **Looked after the servers** the sites ran on: security against common threats, regular audits and updates, and high uptime.
- **Optimized for accessibility and search**, so the sites worked for everyone and could be found.
