From June 2019 to February 2021 I was a web developer and designer at Bartlett Interactive, a small agency in Concord, Massachusetts, that builds websites for nonprofits and local organizations around Boston. Working alongside designers, project managers, senior developers and a wide range of clients, I learned how to build for real people, and how the business around the work runs.

## Getting started

I joined in the spring of 2019, just out of UMass Amherst. I'd designed and built web applications in class, from wireframes to working interfaces, but never for a real client. A summer internship at Bartlett was the chance to do that.

On my first day I shadowed the agency's founder as he pitched the Brookline Education Foundation, a nonprofit that supports excellence in Brookline's public schools. They wanted a new site that would make donating easier, bring in more community involvement, and take grant applications for new learning programs.

Over the next few weeks I worked closely with their directors and volunteers to design and build it, on a custom WordPress theme made for what they needed.

## Going full-time

After the internship I was offered a full-time role. To keep costs down for clients, I started running my own projects end to end: pitching to prospective clients, building relationships with the people involved, planning the work, estimating costs, and launching on time and on budget. I was a developer first, but I learned the business side along the way.

- **Delivered more than 30 web projects** for Boston-area nonprofits, often on tight deadlines.
- **Built responsive, CMS-driven sites** in WordPress with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP, with custom front-end components and content clients could edit themselves.
- **Looked after the servers** the sites ran on: security, regular audits and updates, and high uptime.
- **Built for accessibility and search,** so the sites worked for everyone and could be found.

My clients included the Charlotte and William Bloomberg Medford Public Library, the Lucius Beebe Memorial Library, Somerville Public Library, Brookline Arts Center, the Brookline Innovation Fund, Cognoa, Go Healthcare Staffing, and the Northeast Energy Efficiency Partnerships: organizations working on public health and education, which I was proud to help get online.
