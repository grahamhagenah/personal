From 2019 to 2021 I designed and built websites for nonprofits, libraries and public-interest organizations around Boston, as a developer and designer at Bartlett Interactive, an agency in Concord, Massachusetts. I ran my projects end to end: scoping and estimates, design, development, launch and ongoing support.

## What I delivered

- **More than 30 websites,** launched on time and within budget.
- **Custom WordPress themes** built to each client's needs, with content their own staff could update.
- **Accessible, search-friendly sites** that worked for every visitor and could be found.
- **Reliable hosting,** with security, regular updates and high uptime.

## Clients

The Brookline Education Foundation, the Charlotte and William Bloomberg Medford Public Library, the Lucius Beebe Memorial Library, Somerville Public Library, Brookline Arts Center, the Brookline Innovation Fund, Cognoa, Go Healthcare Staffing, and the Northeast Energy Efficiency Partnerships.
